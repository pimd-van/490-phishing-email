Email about an account, order or problem from a company. Phishing emails often tell a believable story: suspicious activity on your account, a billing problem, a refund or coupon, an unpaid invoice, or a request to confirm your details. Ask yourself whether you actually have an account with this company and whether you know the sender. If not, do not click, reply or open attachments; delete or report it. If you do use the company, do not use the links or phone numbers in the message. Find its contact details yourself, from a statement, a card or by typing the official address, and ask whether the problem exists.
