Job offer that asks you to pay, cash a check, or forward items or money. Treat a job offer by email as a scam if it promises high pay for little work or asks you to pay for training, certification or equipment, because legitimate employers do not charge to hire. Other warning signs are a request to deposit a check and send part of it back, to buy gift cards, or to receive and reship packages. Confirm the opening on the employer's official website, search the company name with words like scam or complaint, and talk to someone you trust. If you already paid, contact the payment provider at once and report the fraud.
