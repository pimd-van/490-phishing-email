Investment or stock tip promising big or quick profits. Be suspicious of any message that promises large or fast profits from a stock, forex, crypto or other investment, or that treats risk as unimportant. Every investment carries risk, and scammers may show fake proof of gains after you put money in. Do not reply or invest because of an unsolicited tip. Search the company and the people promoting it with words like review, scam or complaint, and check your country's securities regulator register to see whether they are licensed. Report suspected investment scams to your national fraud-reporting service.
