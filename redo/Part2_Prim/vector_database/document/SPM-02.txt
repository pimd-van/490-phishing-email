Unsubscribe link or button in a marketing or suspicious email. Only unsubscribe from a message that comes from a company you recognise and have dealt with, and do it through your email provider's own unsubscribe button or settings instead of links inside the message. If you are not sure the sender is genuine, do not click anything, including an unsubscribe link. Mark the message as spam or report it as phishing, then delete it.
