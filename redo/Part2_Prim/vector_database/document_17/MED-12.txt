Email selling medicine or inviting you to order from an online pharmacy. Unsolicited emails offering prescription drugs with no prescription, very large discounts or shipping from abroad are warning signs of unsafe online pharmacies; the products may be fake or contaminated and your card details may be misused. Do not buy through the email. If you need medicine online, use a pharmacy that requires a prescription, lists a real address and phone number, is licensed in your country and has a pharmacist you can speak to. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist, and report bad sellers to your health authority.
