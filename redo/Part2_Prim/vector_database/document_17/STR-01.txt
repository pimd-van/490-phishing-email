A stranger offers you money, an inheritance or a relationship. A message from a stranger may promise a large sum, an inheritance, a share of a fortune, or a romantic friendship. Scammers then ask for money or personal details for travel, medical bills or other emergencies, often by wire transfer, gift card or cryptocurrency, and they push you to act quickly. Never send money, gifts or personal details to someone you have not met in person, and do not reply. Tell someone you trust. If you already paid, contact your bank or the payment company at once, and report the message to your country's fraud service.
