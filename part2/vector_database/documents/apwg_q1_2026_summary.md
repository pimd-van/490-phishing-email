# APWG Phishing Activity Trends Report, 1st Quarter 2026: original summary

This file is an original, source-grounded summary of the identified APWG report, not a reproduction of the copyrighted report.

The Anti-Phishing Working Group published its first-quarter 2026 Phishing Activity Trends Report on May 21, 2026. It covers activity from January through March 2026 and draws on reports from APWG member companies, research partners, the public reporting website, and email submissions. APWG distinguishes unique phishing sites from individual URLs, tracks distinct phishing email subjects as a rough measure of lure variety, and normalizes brand names when counting targeted brands.

APWG reported 971,181 phishing attacks in Q1 2026, up 13.8 percent from 853,244 in Q4 2025. It identified 766 unique targeted brands during the quarter. Telecom was the most-targeted sector at 33 percent, followed by SaaS/webmail at 20 percent. APWG also reported growth in telephone-based fraud and described impersonation and scams as the dominant threat categories observed on social platforms. The report cautions that email-system blocking can reduce the number of lure messages forwarded to APWG, affecting one of its collection channels.

The APWG trends page has since published a Q2 2026 summary. This file intentionally represents the specifically identified Q1 2026 report and its publication date; it does not extrapolate its figures to later quarters.
