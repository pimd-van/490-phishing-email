# NIST Phish Scale User Guide (TN 2276): original summary

This file is an original, source-grounded summary rather than a reproduction of the NIST Phish Scale or its worksheets.

NIST Technical Note 2276 describes the Phish Scale as a method for phishing-awareness training implementers to rate how difficult a simulated phishing email is for a person to detect. The guide is meant to add context to exercise click rates: results should be interpreted alongside the difficulty of the message, rather than assuming every simulated message posed the same detection challenge. NIST says the method considers characteristics of the email and the user context of its recipient. The publication includes instructions for applying the method, worksheets, and supporting discussion of email properties and related research.

The publication page identifies the final document date as November 15, 2023 and provides DOI 10.6028/NIST.TN.2276. NIST’s current Human-Centered Cybersecurity page adds an important licensing condition: academic use is available at no cost, but organizations must establish a research license agreement with NIST; commercial applications require a commercialization license. Consult NIST’s Technology Partnerships Office before applying or redistributing the method. This summary does not reproduce the scoring method or worksheets.
