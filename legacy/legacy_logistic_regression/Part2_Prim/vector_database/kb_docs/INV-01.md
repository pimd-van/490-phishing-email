## Text

> Be suspicious of any message that promises large or fast profits from a stock, forex, crypto or other investment, or that treats risk as unimportant. Every investment carries risk, and scammers may show fake proof of gains after you put money in. Do not reply or invest because of an unsolicited tip. Search the company and the people promoting it with words like review, scam or complaint, and check your country's securities regulator register to see whether they are licensed. Report suspected investment scams to your national fraud-reporting service.

## When to assign (labeling rules v1.5)

- **Assign when:** Stock, penny-stock, crypto or forex tips, "target price", "get in now", promised returns
- **Do not assign when:** Loans and mortgages (not covered)
- **Fit:** Guarantee, target price or clear call to act = good; news and price only = partial.
