Unwanted bulk email, or spam that looks like a scam. For unwanted bulk email, use your email service's own tools: mark messages as spam or junk so the filter learns, and block senders that keep writing. If a message looks like a scam or the spam keeps coming, report it through your provider's spam or phishing option and to a national reporting service if your country has one. Do not reply, and never click links or open files in suspicious bulk email, because that is how many scams and infections start. Keep your devices updated and run security software.
